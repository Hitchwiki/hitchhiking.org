Nostr on the road

Your identity, wherever you travel.

Nostr is an open protocol for carrying your identity and social connections between independent apps.

NIP-07 lets a browser extension hold your keys and approve signatures, so this page never needs your private key. We use it to detect your public identity and check whether it has a verified Trustroots NIP-05 address.
